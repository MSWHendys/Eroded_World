# Eroded World

**A hardcore survival overhaul for Minecraft Fabric.**

*The world remembers your actions and reacts to your decisions.*

Eroded World transforms Minecraft survival into a more challenging experience. Mining destabilizes the underground, darkness becomes dangerous, energy affects survival, and the environment responds to player activity.

## Main Features

- **Energy & Stamina** – Sprinting, mining, crafting, repairs and dodging consume energy.
- **Mining & Cave-ins** – Underground activity can destabilize tunnels and trigger collapses.
- **True Darkness** – Unlit environments become a genuine survival threat.
- **Dynamic Ecosystem** – Nature reacts to player activity and long-term environmental damage.
- **Territory & Threats** – Local activity affects danger levels and environmental conditions.
- **Crafting Quality** – Equipment quality influences survival and progression.
- **Territory Protection** – Protect bases and land using anchors and configurable permissions.
- **Death Recovery** – Recover lost equipment using Death Chests and Death Compasses.
- **Dangerous Enemies** – Hostile creatures become more threatening as the environment deteriorates.
- **Survival Items** – Special consumables and equipment help players survive difficult situations.

## Source Code & Development Branches

**The `main` branch is the central project overview, not a version-specific source distribution.**

Source code, dependencies and build instructions are maintained in separate branches corresponding to Minecraft versions.

**[Browse all source branches](https://github.com/MSWHendys/Eroded_World/branches)**

To build Eroded World from source:

1. Select the branch matching your target Minecraft version.
2. Read that branch's version-specific README.
3. Install the required Java version and development dependencies.
4. Follow the build instructions provided in that branch.

Older releases, including version 1.1.0, are preserved in the project's development history and version-specific branches.
